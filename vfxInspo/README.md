# vfxInspo — VFX style references

Reference frames for the look-and-feel of HyperFrames video scenes. Each filename
describes the visual technique so an LLM authoring a composition can pick the right
inspiration quickly.

## Frames

- **torn-paper-article-clippings-on-graph-grid-with-centered-logo-cutout.png**
  Layered torn-paper article clippings (serif body text, ripped/perforated edges)
  floating over a cream graph-paper grid. A centered card holds a logo cutout that
  occludes the clippings behind it. Subtle drop shadows sell the paper depth.
  Good for: "explainer source material" reveals, document call-outs, topic intros.

- **article-screenshot-with-animated-yellow-highlighter-marker-sweep.png**
  A real article/browser screenshot on a light card, with key phrases revealed by
  animated yellow highlighter marker-sweeps (hand-drawn, slightly uneven edges).
  Good for: citing sources, emphasizing a quote, walking through a ruling/claim.

- **vintage-polaroid-photo-cards-aged-torn-edges-with-handdrawn-sketch-on-grid.png**
  Vintage polaroid/photo cards with aged, torn, scuffed edges and a taped paper
  caption label, paired with hand-drawn line-sketch counterparts, scattered over a
  grid background. Good for: historical/retro beats, "early prototype" reveals,
  before/after sketch-to-product transitions.

- **dark-mode-paper-text-with-amber-glow-highlight-focus-and-red-ambient-vignette.png**
  A dark-mode academic-paper passage zoomed in close, with an animated amber/gold
  highlight-glow sweeping over a key sentence, a soft vignette darkening the edges,
  and a red ambient glow bleeding in from the top. The glow reads as a warm spotlight
  rather than a flat marker. Good for: citing research/stats, focusing one line in a
  dense paper, "the key finding is..." reveals on a dark theme.

- **printed-article-on-wooden-desk-perspective-tilt.png**
  A printed Medium-style blog article on a white sheet lying flat on a warm wooden
  desk, shot at a perspective tilt (top edge receding) with a soft vignette. Reads as
  a real-world tabletop scene. Good for: "I read this article" beats, source intros,
  grounding a screenshot in a physical, lived-in space. First beat of the pair below.

- **printed-article-on-wooden-desk-with-polaroid-event-photos-dropping-in.png**
  Same wooden-desk article scene, second beat: polaroid event photos drop/scatter in
  on top of the article at jaunty angles with drop shadows. Good for: layering
  supporting imagery onto a source, "and here's what it looked like" reveals,
  collage build-ons over a base document.

- **low-angle-skyscraper-hero-shot-on-blue-cloudy-sky-with-serif-title-building-in.png**
  Cinematic low-angle hero shot of a tiered glass-and-stone skyscraper against a deep
  blue sky with drifting clouds; a serif headline begins to build in over the sky
  (here just the first word). Slow push / parallax feel. Good for: grand "this became
  huge" statements, company/era establishing shots, epic chapter openers. First beat
  of the pair below.

- **low-angle-skyscraper-hero-shot-with-full-serif-headline-overlay-on-sky.png**
  Same skyscraper hero shot, second beat: the full multi-line serif headline has
  faded/typed in across the sky above the building, set in the negative space. Good
  for: the payoff line of an establishing shot, title cards that sit in real scenery
  rather than a flat card.

- **architecture-flow-diagram-on-curled-corner-paper-sheet-on-textured-blue-bg.png**
  A labeled box-and-arrow architecture/flow diagram (colored nodes, dotted + solid
  labeled arrows) rendered on a white paper sheet with a curled top-right corner,
  tilted on a textured blue background with a soft drop shadow. Good for: explaining
  a system/data flow, MVVM-style relationships, "here's how the pieces talk" beats —
  the paper-sheet framing keeps a dry diagram feeling tactile.

- **scattered-research-collage-dark-text-cards-bw-portrait-diagram-on-grid-with-amber-highlight.png**
  A pinboard-style research collage on a graph-paper grid: several dark article cards
  (serif text), a cut-out black-and-white portrait on a white card, and a small color
  diagram card, all layered at jaunty angles with drop shadows. An amber highlight
  glow marks one key line. Good for: "here's the source material" deep-dive intros,
  attributing an idea to a person, mosaic of evidence beats that mix text + face +
  diagram in one frame.

- **triptych-of-monochrome-diagram-cards-in-a-row-on-graph-grid.png**
  Three white textured square cards evenly spaced in a row, each holding a clean
  monochrome technical diagram (flowchart, box-and-arrow architecture), on a
  graph-paper grid background. A calm gallery/triptych layout. Good for: comparing
  three approaches side by side, "there are three ways to do this" beats, sequential
  card-by-card reveals where each diagram steps forward in turn.

- **blueprint-grid-scene-two-phones-connected-by-dashed-line-through-labeled-pill-node.png**
  A blueprint-schematic scene: deep-blue blueprint grid with a ruler-tick border, two
  devices (a modern phone left, an older phone right) connected by an animated dashed
  line that runs through a green labeled pill node in the middle. A staggered serif
  headline floats above. Good for: "A became B" / migration & lineage beats, showing
  two things bridged by a connector, old-vs-new comparisons with a labeled link.

- **fullscreen-newspaper-article-serif-headline-body-with-blue-ruler-header-scroll-start.png**
  A full-screen newspaper/print article: a big bold serif headline over justified
  serif body columns on an off-white paper texture, with a blue ruler-tick header bar
  across the top. First beat of an auto-scroll. Good for: long-form "the story so far"
  reveals, faux editorial framing, headline-then-body builds. Pairs with the frame
  below.

- **fullscreen-newspaper-article-auto-scrolling-body-copy-ruler-scrolled-past.png**
  Same newspaper article, mid-scroll: the ruler header has scrolled off and the body
  copy has crept up to reveal later paragraphs. Good for: showing a slow vertical
  auto-scroll through dense copy while narration reads along, "...and it kept going"
  continuation beats.

- **torn-paper-eyebrow-headline-card-carousel-white-problem-card-over-blurred-green-red-bokeh.png**
  A horizontal carousel of torn-paper cards (ripped bottom edge, drop shadow) sliding
  across a heavily blurred green-and-red bokeh background. The centered white card holds
  a small grey eyebrow label ("PROBLEM") above a bold black sans headline; neighbor cards
  peek in at the frame edges. Good for: punchy "here's the setup" statement beats, swipe/
  slide transitions between linked claims, label-then-headline reveals. First beat of the
  pair below.

- **torn-paper-eyebrow-headline-card-carousel-red-consequence-card-over-blurred-green-red-bokeh.png**
  Same torn-paper carousel, next beat: a red card has slid into center with a "CONSEQUENCE"
  eyebrow and bold black headline, the previous white card now peeking off-frame. Good for:
  the payoff/consequence half of a problem→result pair, color-shift emphasis (white→red as
  stakes rise), carousel advance beats where each card escalates the last.

- **tilted-phone-app-mockup-hero-over-dark-green-red-blurred-bokeh.png**
  A single phone device mockup (here an e-commerce "Explore" shopping app) floating
  centered and slightly tilted as a hero, over a dark, moody green-and-red blurred bokeh
  background with a soft vignette. Reads as a product showcase. Good for: "here's the app"
  product intros, feature spotlights on one screen, grounding a UI in an atmospheric
  cinematic backdrop rather than a flat slide.

- **phone-mockup-with-dashed-curves-to-labeled-module-pills-topped-by-user-icon-rows-on-green-gradient.png**
  A phone mockup anchored at bottom-center on a dark green radial gradient, with animated
  curved dashed lines fanning out to several labeled dark module pills ("User Authentication
  Module", "Category Module", "Search & Filter Module"), each pill topped by a row of white
  user/person icons. A system/dependency diagram built around a real device. Good for:
  "one app, many moving parts" breakdowns, showing modules/teams/users connected to a
  product, architecture-around-a-screen explainers.

- **cinematic-hooded-figure-at-night-window-with-paper-card-black-marker-highlighted-term-list.png**
  A cinematic illustrated scene: a hooded figure shot from behind at a desk, gazing out a
  rain-streaked window over a blue night cityscape. A white paper card overlays the lower
  half with body text where a run of terms is struck through with thick black marker
  highlight ("Device Variance, Backward Compatibility, Build Systems, Architecture Patterns…").
  Good for: "they had to think about all of THIS" overwhelm beats, dramatic story framing of
  a hard problem, black-marker term-list reveals over a moody narrative backdrop.

- **silhouette-head-profile-with-white-brain-and-tangled-orange-scribble-glow-thoughts-on-dark.png**
  A dark grey head silhouette in profile holding a clean white line-drawn brain, with a
  dense tangle of hand-drawn orange scribbles erupting from the skull and glowing against a
  near-black background. The chaotic scrawl reads as racing, overwhelming thought. Good for:
  "too much going on in your head" / cognitive-overload beats, anxiety or confusion moments,
  the messy-thinking half of a clarity before/after.
